9.01

Recognize and classify liabilities確認及分類負債

A liability is a present obligation from past events. Current liabilities are generally due within one year or the operating cycle, whichever is longer; classify the current portion of long-term debt separately. 負債是由過去事項產生的現有義務；一般在一年或營運週期（較長者）內到期的屬流動負債。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

A three-year loan has 10,000 principal due next year and 40,000 later: report 10,000 current, 40,000 long-term.

YOUR TURN · 練習

A company owes 12,000 due in 8 months and 50,000 due in 3 years. Classify.

8 個月後到期 12,000；3 年後到期 50,000。如何分類？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

12,000 current; 50,000 non-current, assuming a normal operating cycle. 流動 12,000；非流動 50,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

9.02

Account for notes payable and interest記錄應付票據與利息

Interest = principal × annual rate × time in years. Accrue interest at period end; pay principal plus remaining interest at maturity. 利息＝本金 × 年利率 × 年份比例；期末須計提未付利息。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Borrow 100,000 at 6% on Nov 1; at Dec 31 accrue 100,000 × 6% × 2/12 = 1,000: Dr Interest Expense; Cr Interest Payable.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Borrow 60,000 at 8% on Oct 1. How much interest accrues by Dec 31?

10 月 1 日以年利率 8% 借入 60,000。12 月 31 日應計利息多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

60,000 × 8% × 3/12 = 1,200. 應計利息 1,200。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

9.03

Account for contingencies and warranties處理或有負債與保養

Under the slides’ US GAAP approach, accrue an estimated loss if probable and reasonably estimable; disclose certain other material uncertainties. Estimate warranty expense in the sales period and reduce the liability when repairs occur. 按課堂美國準則，損失很可能發生且能合理估計時入賬；其他重大不確定性視情況披露。保養成本於銷售期估計。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Sales 200,000, expected warranty cost 2% → Dr Warranty Expense 4,000; Cr Warranty Liability 4,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Sales 300,000, expected warranty cost 3%; actual repair costs 2,000. What remains in liability?

銷售額 300,000，預計保養成本 3%；實際維修成本 2,000。負債餘額多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Accrue 9,000; subtract repairs 2,000; liability 7,000. 計提 9,000，餘額 7,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

9.04

Calculate present and future value計算現值與終值

FV = PV(1 + i)^n; PV = FV/(1 + i)^n. Match the interest rate and number of periods to compounding frequency. Ordinary-annuity PV = payment × [1 − (1+i)^−n]/i, with payments at each period end. 終值＝現值×(1+i)^n；現值則折現。利率與期數須使用相同期間；普通年金於每期末付款。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Receive 1,100 one year from now at 10% → PV = 1,000. Two year-end receipts of 100 at 10% → PV ≈ 173.55.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Receive 1,210 in two years; discount rate 10% annually. Present value?

兩年後收取 1,210，年折現率 10%。現值多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

1,210 / 1.1² = 1,000. 現值 1,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

9.05

Distinguish secured and unsecured debt區分有抵押及無抵押債務

Secured debt is backed by specified collateral; unsecured debt relies on the borrower’s general creditworthiness. A long-term obligation can include a current portion. 有抵押債務以特定資產作擔保；無抵押債務依賴借款人的整體信貸。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

A mortgage on a building gives the lender a claim against the pledged property.

YOUR TURN · 練習

A company borrows without pledging any specific asset. What type of debt?

借款時沒有抵押任何特定資產。這是甚麼債務？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Unsecured debt. 無抵押債務。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？
