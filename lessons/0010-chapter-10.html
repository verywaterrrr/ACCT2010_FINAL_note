10.01

Explain bond terms and cash flows解釋債券條款及現金流

Face value is repaid at maturity. Stated rate determines cash interest; market rate determines the discount rate and issue price. Use rate per payment period and total number of periods. 面值於到期償還；票面利率決定現金利息，市場利率決定發行價格。使用每期利率及總期數。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

100,000 face, 8% stated, semiannual payments → cash interest 4,000 every six months.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Face 200,000, stated 6%, semiannual interest. Cash paid each period?

面值 200,000，票面年利率 6%，每半年付息。每期現金利息多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

200,000 × 6% ÷ 2 = 6,000. 每半年 6,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

10.02

Price bonds and identify discount or premium計算債券價格並判斷折價或溢價

Issue price = PV of principal + PV of coupon payments at market rate. Market > stated → discount; market < stated → premium; equal → par. 發行價＝本金現值＋利息現值。市場利率高於票面利率→折價；低於→溢價。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

One-year 100,000 bond, 6% coupon, 10% market: (100,000 + 6,000)/1.10 = 96,363.64 discount.

YOUR TURN · 練習

A bond pays 5% when market rate is 7%. Will it issue above or below face?

票面利率 5%，市場利率 7%。發行價高於還是低於面值？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Below face value, at a discount. 折價發行。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

10.03

Record bond issue and coupon payments記錄債券發行及利息支付

At par: Dr Cash, Cr Bonds Payable at face. For a discount, debit Discount on Bonds Payable; for a premium, credit Premium on Bonds Payable. At each payment recognize interest expense and cash paid. 平價發行借記現金、貸記應付債券；折價借記折價，溢價貸記溢價。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Issue face 100,000 for 96,000: Dr Cash 96,000; Dr Discount 4,000; Cr Bonds Payable 100,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Issue 100,000 face for 103,000. State entry.

以 103,000 發行面值 100,000 的債券。寫出分錄。

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Dr Cash 103,000; Cr Bonds Payable 100,000; Cr Premium 3,000. 借現金，貸債券及溢價。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

10.04

Amortize using effective interest使用實際利率法攤銷

Interest expense = beginning carrying value × market rate per period. Discount amortization = expense − cash interest; premium amortization = cash interest − expense. Carrying amount moves toward face value. 利息費用＝期初賬面值×每期市場利率；折價攤銷＝費用－現金利息，溢價則相反。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Face 100,000, carrying 96,000, stated 6%, market 8%, annual: expense 7,680; cash 6,000; discount amortized 1,680; ending carrying value 97,680.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Face 100,000, carrying 105,000, stated 8%, market 6%, annual. Expense and ending carrying value?

面值 100,000，賬面值 105,000，票面利率 8%，市場利率 6%，每年付息。利息費用及期末賬面值多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Expense 6,300; cash 8,000; premium amortized 1,700; carrying value 103,300. 利息費用 6,300；期末賬面值 103,300。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

10.05

Apply straight-line amortization and retirement直線法攤銷及提前償還

Straight-line amortization allocates total discount or premium evenly across interest periods. On early retirement, gain/loss = carrying amount − repurchase price; remove both face and unamortized discount/premium. 直線法將折溢價平均攤分各付息期；提前償還損益＝賬面值－買回價。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Discount 4,000 over 4 annual periods → 1,000/year. Retire carrying value 98,000 for 99,000 → loss 1,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

A bond carrying value is 102,000 and repurchase costs 100,500. Gain or loss?

債券賬面值 102,000，買回價 100,500。收益還是損失？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Gain 1,500. 償還收益 1,500。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

10.06

Interpret debt-to-equity解讀負債權益比率

Debt-to-equity = total liabilities ÷ total stockholders’ equity. A higher ratio suggests greater financial leverage; interpretation depends on the industry. 負債權益比率＝總負債 ÷ 股東權益總額；比率越高代表財務槓桿較高。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Liabilities 600,000 and equity 300,000 → 2.0.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Liabilities 450,000 and equity 600,000. Ratio?

總負債 450,000，權益 600,000。比率多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

0.75. 負債權益比率 0.75。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？
