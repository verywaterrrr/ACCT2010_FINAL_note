8.01

Classify and measure acquisition cost分類及計量購入成本

Capitalize expenditures needed to acquire the asset and bring it into use; routine repairs are expensed. Land is not depreciated. 從購入到資產可供使用的必要成本資本化；日常維修列費用。土地不折舊。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Machine $50,000 + freight $2,000 + installation $3,000 = asset cost $55,000. 機器入賬成本為 $55,000。

YOUR TURN · 練習

A machine costs $30,000; delivery $1,000; annual maintenance $500. What is capitalized?

一台機器售價 $30,000，運費 $1,000，年度保養費 $500。應資本化多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

The asset cost is $31,000; maintenance $500 is an expense. 資產成本 $31,000；維修費 $500。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

8.02

Separate capital expenditure from expense區分資本支出與費用

A later cost that improves capacity, extends useful life, or improves quality is generally capitalized; ordinary maintenance is expensed. 延長壽命、提升產能或質素的支出通常資本化；一般保養列費用。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

A $4,000 upgrade extends a machine’s life: Dr Equipment 4,000; Cr Cash 4,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

A $600 oil change maintains normal performance. Asset or expense?

更換機油花費 $600，維持正常性能。應列資產還是費用？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Expense $600: it maintains existing operating condition. 費用 $600。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

8.03

Compute straight-line depreciation計算直線法折舊

Annual depreciation = (cost − residual value) ÷ useful life. Accumulated depreciation is a contra-asset; book value = cost − accumulated depreciation. 年折舊＝（成本－殘值）÷ 使用年期；賬面值＝成本－累計折舊。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Cost 50,000, residual 5,000, life 5 years → annual depreciation 9,000. Dr Depreciation Expense 9,000; Cr Accumulated Depreciation 9,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Cost 34,000, residual 4,000, life 5 years. Find annual depreciation and end-year-1 book value.

成本 34,000、殘值 4,000、使用年期 5 年。求年度折舊及第一年末賬面值。

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Depreciation 6,000; book value 28,000. 年折舊 6,000，年末賬面值 28,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

8.04

Compute units-of-production depreciation計算產量法折舊

Rate per unit = (cost − residual value) ÷ estimated total units; expense = rate × actual units produced. 每單位折舊＝（成本－殘值）÷ 預計總產量；本期折舊＝單位折舊 × 實際產量。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Cost 50,000, residual 5,000, total 90,000 units: $0.50 per unit. Production 12,000 → expense 6,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Cost 84,000, residual 4,000, expected 40,000 units; actual 5,000 units. Expense?

成本 84,000、殘值 4,000、預計總產量 40,000 件，本期生產 5,000 件。折舊多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Rate $2 per unit; depreciation $10,000. 每單位 $2；折舊 $10,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

8.05

Compute double-declining-balance depreciation計算雙倍餘額遞減法折舊

Rate = 2 ÷ useful life; multiply beginning book value by that rate. Do not reduce the ending book value below residual value. 折舊率＝2 ÷ 年期；乘期初賬面值，最低保留殘值。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Cost 50,000, residual 5,000, life 5 years: rate 40%; year 1 expense 20,000; year 2 expense 12,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Cost 20,000, residual 2,000, life 4 years. Find the first two years’ expense.

成本 20,000、殘值 2,000、年期 4 年。求首兩年折舊。

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

50% rate; year 1 = 10,000 and year 2 = 5,000. 第一年 10,000；第二年 5,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

8.06

Revise estimates and assess impairment修訂估計及評估減值

A change in estimate applies prospectively: spread remaining depreciable book value over the revised remaining life. For the course’s US GAAP model, test recoverability using undiscounted future cash flows; if book value exceeds them, loss = book value − fair value. 估計變更向前處理。課堂的美國準則模型先以未折現未來現金流測試可收回性，減值損失＝賬面值－公允價值。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Book value 60,000; residual 6,000; revised remaining life 3 years → 18,000/year. Separately, book 60,000 > future cash 50,000; fair value 42,000 → impairment 18,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Book value 80,000, undiscounted cash flows 75,000, fair value 65,000. Loss?

賬面值 80,000，未折現未來現金流 75,000，公允價值 65,000。減值損失多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Recoverability fails; loss = 80,000 − 65,000 = 15,000. 減值損失 15,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

8.07

Record asset disposals記錄資產處置

Update depreciation to the disposal date. Remove cost and accumulated depreciation; gain/loss = proceeds − book value. 先補提折舊，再沖銷成本與累計折舊；處置損益＝所得款項－賬面值。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Cost 40,000, accumulated depreciation 28,000, sale proceeds 15,000 → book value 12,000; gain 3,000. Dr Cash 15,000, Dr Accumulated Depreciation 28,000; Cr Equipment 40,000, Cr Gain 3,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Cost 30,000, accumulated depreciation 22,000, proceeds 6,000. Gain or loss?

成本 30,000，累計折舊 22,000，出售所得 6,000。收益還是損失？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Book value 8,000; loss 2,000. 賬面值 8,000；損失 2,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

8.08

Explain intangibles, goodwill and depletion解釋無形資產、商譽及耗竭

Purchased finite-life intangibles are amortized over useful life; indefinite-life assets and goodwill are tested for impairment rather than amortized. Goodwill on acquisition = purchase price − fair value of identifiable net assets. Natural resources use depletion as extracted. 購入的有限年期無形資產須攤銷；無限年期資產與商譽須減值測試。商譽＝收購價－可辨認淨資產公允價值；天然資源按開採量計耗竭。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Buy a business for 900,000 with identifiable net assets worth 720,000 → goodwill 180,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Pay 500,000 for a business with identifiable net assets at fair value of 420,000. Goodwill?

以 500,000 購入企業，可辨認淨資產公允價值 420,000。商譽多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

80,000; internally generated reputation is not separately recorded as goodwill. 商譽 80,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

8.09

Interpret fixed-asset turnover解讀固定資產周轉率

Fixed-asset turnover = net sales ÷ average net fixed assets; it measures sales generated per dollar invested in fixed assets. Compare similar firms and periods. 固定資產周轉率＝淨銷售額 ÷ 平均固定資產淨額，衡量資產創造銷售額的效率。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Sales 600,000; average net fixed assets 200,000 → 3 times.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Sales 900,000; beginning and ending net fixed assets 250,000 and 350,000. Turnover?

銷售額 900,000，期初及期末固定資產淨額分別為 250,000 及 350,000。周轉率多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Average 300,000; turnover 3.0 times. 平均 300,000，周轉率 3 次。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？
