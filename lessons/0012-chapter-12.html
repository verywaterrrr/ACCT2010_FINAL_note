12.01

Classify operating, investing and financing cash flows分類經營、投資及籌資現金流

Operating activities concern normal income-generating operations; investing includes purchases/sales of long-term assets; financing involves owners and creditors. Under the course’s US GAAP presentation, interest paid and dividends received are operating, dividends paid are financing. 經營活動涉及日常營運；投資活動涉及長期資產；籌資活動涉及股東及債權人。按課堂美國準則，支付利息及收到股利屬經營，支付股利屬籌資。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Cash from customers 50,000 = operating; buy equipment 10,000 = investing outflow; borrow 20,000 = financing inflow.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Classify: issue shares, sell equipment, pay employees.

將發行股票、出售設備、支付員工工資分類。

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Financing inflow; investing inflow; operating outflow. 籌資流入、投資流入、經營流出。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

12.02

Reconcile cash and disclose noncash transactions核對現金及披露非現金交易

Net cash change = operating + investing + financing; beginning cash + change = ending cash. Significant noncash investing/financing transactions are disclosed separately. 現金淨變動＝三類現金流總和；期初現金＋變動＝期末現金。重大非現金投資與籌資另行披露。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Operating +20,000, investing −8,000, financing +3,000 → cash rises 15,000. Buying equipment by issuing shares is disclosed separately.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Beginning cash 5,000; O +12,000, I −9,000, F −2,000. Ending cash?

期初現金 5,000；經營 +12,000、投資 −9,000、籌資 −2,000。期末現金多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

6,000. 期末現金 6,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

12.03

Reconcile net income using the indirect method用間接法將淨利潤調整為經營現金流

Start with net income; add noncash expenses and losses; subtract gains. Add decreases in operating current assets and increases in operating current liabilities; subtract increases in operating current assets and decreases in operating current liabilities. 從淨利潤開始，加回非現金費用及損失，減去收益；經營流動資產減少及流動負債增加加回，反之減去。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

NI 30,000 + depreciation 5,000 − gain 2,000 − increase in receivables 4,000 + increase in payables 3,000 = CFO 32,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

NI 20,000; depreciation 4,000; receivables increase 3,000; payables decrease 1,000. CFO?

淨利潤 20,000；折舊 4,000；應收賬款增加 3,000；應付賬款減少 1,000。經營現金流多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

20,000 + 4,000 − 3,000 − 1,000 = 20,000. 經營現金流 20,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

12.04

Calculate investing cash flows計算投資活動現金流

Record cash paid for acquisition and cash received on sale of long-term investments or assets. The full proceeds from sale go in investing; gain/loss is adjusted out of indirect operating cash flow. 購買及出售長期資產的實際現金列入投資活動；全部出售所得列投資，處置損益則於間接法經營活動調整。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Sell equipment with book value 8,000 for 11,000: investing inflow 11,000; subtract gain 3,000 from net income in CFO.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Sell equipment for 7,000 with book value 9,000. Investing cash flow and indirect adjustment?

設備以 7,000 出售，賬面值 9,000。投資現金流及間接法調整各是多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Investing inflow 7,000; add back loss 2,000 to net income. 投資流入 7,000；加回損失 2,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

12.05

Calculate financing cash flows計算籌資活動現金流

Cash from issuing shares or borrowing is an inflow; cash to repay principal, repurchase shares, or pay dividends is an outflow. Borrowing principal is distinct from interest. 發行股票及借款為流入；償還本金、購回股票及支付股利為流出。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Borrow 40,000, repay principal 10,000, pay dividends 5,000 → financing cash flow +25,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Issue shares for 20,000, repurchase shares for 3,000 and pay dividends 2,000. Net financing?

發行股票收 20,000、購回股票付 3,000、支付股利 2,000。籌資現金流淨額多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

+15,000. 籌資現金淨流入 15,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

12.06

Prepare operating cash flow using the direct method用直接法編製經營現金流

Show operating cash receipts and payments directly. Cash collected from customers = sales revenue − increase in accounts receivable (add a decrease). With simple inventory and accounts-payable assumptions, purchases = COGS + increase in inventory; cash paid to suppliers = purchases − increase in accounts payable. 直接法列示現金收入與支出。收客現金＝銷售收入－應收賬款增加；簡化情況下，購貨＝銷貨成本＋存貨增加，支付供應商＝購貨－應付賬款增加。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Sales 100,000; A/R rises 5,000 → collections 95,000. COGS 60,000; inventory rises 4,000; A/P rises 2,000 → supplier payments 62,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Sales 80,000; A/R falls 3,000. Cash collected?

銷售收入 80,000，應收賬款減少 3,000。收客現金多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

83,000. 收客現金 83,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？
