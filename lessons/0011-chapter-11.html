11.01

Compare ownership forms and share rights比較企業形式及股票權利

Corporations are separate legal entities with limited liability, transferable ownership, and possible double taxation. Common shareholders generally vote and receive residual returns; preferred shares have specified priority. 公司具有獨立法人及有限責任；普通股通常有投票權，優先股享有指定優先權。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

If assets exceed liabilities after settlement, common shareholders have the residual claim after preferred claims.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Which shareholders normally vote and bear the residual risk?

哪類股東通常有投票權並承擔剩餘風險？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Common shareholders. 普通股股東。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

11.02

Track authorized, issued and outstanding shares區分核准、已發行及流通股

Authorized shares are the maximum permitted; issued shares have been sold or distributed; outstanding = issued − treasury shares. 核准股數為可發行上限；已發行股數已售出或分配；流通股＝已發行股－庫存股。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Authorized 1,000,000; issued 400,000; treasury 50,000 → outstanding 350,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Issued 250,000, treasury 30,000. Outstanding?

已發行 250,000 股，庫存股 30,000 股。流通股多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

220,000 shares. 流通股 220,000 股。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

11.03

Record share issuance記錄發行股票

For par-value shares, credit Common Stock for shares × par; credit additional paid-in capital (APIC) for proceeds above par. 股票面值入普通股賬戶；超出面值的款項入額外繳入資本。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Issue 1,000 shares, $2 par, for $10 each: Dr Cash 10,000; Cr Common Stock 2,000; Cr APIC 8,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Issue 2,000 shares of $1 par for $8. What are the credits?

發行 2,000 股，面值 $1，售價 $8。貸記金額分別多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Common Stock 2,000 and APIC 14,000. 普通股 2,000；額外繳入資本 14,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

11.04

Record treasury stock purchase and reissue記錄庫存股購回及再發行

Under the cost method, repurchase debits Treasury Stock, a contra-equity account. Reissue above cost credits APIC—Treasury Stock; reissue below cost debits available APIC—Treasury Stock first, then retained earnings for any remainder. 成本法下購回借記庫存股；高於成本再發行的差額貸記庫存股額外繳入資本；低於成本的差額先抵減該項餘額。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Buy 100 shares at $10: Dr Treasury Stock 1,000. Reissue at $12: Dr Cash 1,200; Cr Treasury Stock 1,000; Cr APIC—Treasury 200.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Buy 100 shares at $10 and reissue at $11. APIC credit?

以每股 $10 購回 100 股，再以 $11 售出。額外繳入資本貸記多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

100 × (11 − 10) = 100. 額外繳入資本 100。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

11.05

Record cash dividends and retained earnings記錄現金股利與留存收益

Declaration creates dividends payable and reduces retained earnings (or a dividends account). Record date creates no entry. Payment reduces cash and payable. Ending retained earnings = beginning + net income − dividends. 宣告日產生應付股利並減少留存收益；記錄日無分錄；支付日減少現金及應付股利。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

Declare 5,000: Dr Retained Earnings 5,000; Cr Dividends Payable 5,000. Pay: Dr Payable 5,000; Cr Cash 5,000.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Beginning retained earnings 40,000, income 12,000, dividends 3,000. Ending balance?

期初留存收益 40,000，淨利潤 12,000，股利 3,000。期末餘額多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

49,000. 留存收益期末 49,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

11.06

Allocate cumulative preferred dividends分配累積優先股股利

Annual preferred dividend = outstanding preferred shares × par × dividend rate. Cumulative unpaid dividends in arrears must be covered before common dividends; arrears are not a liability until declared. 年度優先股股利＝流通優先股數×面值×股息率。累積欠付股利在普通股分配前補付；宣告前不構成負債。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

1,000 shares, $100 par, 5% → 5,000/year. One missed year plus current year requires 10,000 before common.

YOUR TURN · 練習

Annual preferred entitlement 4,000; two prior years unpaid; total declared 15,000. Allocate.

優先股每年應得 4,000，前兩年未付；本年宣告股利總額 15,000。如何分配？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

Preferred 12,000; common 3,000. 優先股 12,000，普通股 3,000。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？

11.07

Compare stock dividends and stock splits比較股票股利與股票分拆

Stock dividends transfer amounts within equity; total equity stays constant. Small dividends are generally recorded at market value; large ones at par in the slides. A stock split increases share count and reduces par per share proportionately. 股票股利於權益內轉撥，總權益不變；小額通常按市值、大額按面值。股票分拆增加股數並按比例降低每股面值。

WORKED EXAMPLE · 示範

10% stock dividend on 1,000 shares adds 100 shares. A 2-for-1 split turns 1,000 $10-par shares into 2,000 $5-par shares.

YOUR TURN · 練習

A 3-for-1 split applies to 600 shares with $9 par. New shares and par?

600 股、每股面值 $9 的股票進行 3 拆 1。新股數及面值多少？

Reveal answer / 顯示答案

1,800 shares; $3 par. 1,800 股，每股面值 $3。

Explain it aloud: why does this treatment make sense? / 試口述：為何這樣處理？
