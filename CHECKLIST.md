# ACCT2010 Final Exam — Post-Midterm Checklist / 期末考核清單

Source boundary: Fall 2026 syllabus and current Chapters 8–12 slides. Chapter 8 is included because the midterm covered Chapters 1–7. The syllabus says the final is cumulative; this checklist intentionally omits Chapters 1–7 at the learner’s request.

The checkbox order below is the lesson order. Check an item when you can explain it and solve a new question independently.

## Chapter 8 — Long-lived assets / 長期資產

- [ ] Classify and measure acquisition cost / 分類及計量購入成本
- [ ] Separate capital expenditure from expense / 區分資本支出與費用
- [ ] Compute straight-line depreciation / 計算直線法折舊
- [ ] Compute units-of-production depreciation / 計算產量法折舊
- [ ] Compute double-declining-balance depreciation / 計算雙倍餘額遞減法折舊
- [ ] Revise estimates and assess impairment / 修訂估計及評估減值
- [ ] Record asset disposals / 記錄資產處置
- [ ] Explain intangibles, goodwill and depletion / 解釋無形資產、商譽及耗竭
- [ ] Interpret fixed-asset turnover / 解讀固定資產周轉率

## Chapter 9 — Liabilities and time value / 負債及貨幣時間價值

- [ ] Recognize and classify liabilities / 確認及分類負債
- [ ] Account for notes payable and interest / 記錄應付票據與利息
- [ ] Account for contingencies and warranties / 處理或有負債與保養
- [ ] Calculate present and future value / 計算現值與終值
- [ ] Distinguish secured and unsecured debt / 區分有抵押及無抵押債務

## Chapter 10 — Bonds / 債券

- [ ] Explain bond terms and cash flows / 解釋債券條款及現金流
- [ ] Price bonds and identify discount or premium / 計算債券價格並判斷折價或溢價
- [ ] Record bond issue and coupon payments / 記錄債券發行及利息支付
- [ ] Amortize using effective interest / 使用實際利率法攤銷
- [ ] Apply straight-line amortization and retirement / 直線法攤銷及提前償還
- [ ] Interpret debt-to-equity / 解讀負債權益比率

## Chapter 11 — Stockholders’ equity / 股東權益

- [ ] Compare ownership forms and share rights / 比較企業形式及股票權利
- [ ] Track authorized, issued and outstanding shares / 區分核准、已發行及流通股
- [ ] Record share issuance / 記錄發行股票
- [ ] Record treasury stock purchase and reissue / 記錄庫存股購回及再發行
- [ ] Record cash dividends and retained earnings / 記錄現金股利與留存收益
- [ ] Allocate cumulative preferred dividends / 分配累積優先股股利
- [ ] Compare stock dividends and stock splits / 比較股票股利與股票分拆

## Chapter 12 — Statement of cash flows / 現金流量表

- [ ] Classify operating, investing and financing cash flows / 分類經營、投資及籌資現金流
- [ ] Reconcile cash and disclose noncash transactions / 核對現金及披露非現金交易
- [ ] Reconcile net income using the indirect method / 用間接法將淨利潤調整為經營現金流
- [ ] Calculate investing cash flows / 計算投資活動現金流
- [ ] Calculate financing cash flows / 計算籌資活動現金流
- [ ] Prepare operating cash flow using the direct method / 用直接法編製經營現金流
